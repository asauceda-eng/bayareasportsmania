Who We Are

Our Mission

Fun, community, and involvement for everyone

At Bay Area Sports Mania, we believe every child — from age 4 to 17 — deserves the chance to discover their passion for sports. We create a positive, inclusive environment where athletes of all skill levels build confidence, make lifelong friendships, and fall in love with the game.


Based in San Jose, we're proud to serve the entire Bay Area community with programs that prioritize fun, safety, and personal growth above everything else.
